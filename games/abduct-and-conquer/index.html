How to play

  1. Abduct. Each night you have 2½ minutes over the countryside. Hold the tractor beam over people, animals and vehicles to pull them into your saucer.
  2. Bank your cargo. The hold fills up. Send cargo to the mothership before your shield breaks, or you lose it.
  3. Build. On the mothership, spend humans, biomass and metal on army units and saucer upgrades.
  4. Invade. Launch the invasion. Destroy the 3 shield pylons, then Earth's HQ. Earth's defenses grow every night you wait.
